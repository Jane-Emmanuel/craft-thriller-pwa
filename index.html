About The Craft Thriller

The Craft Thriller is a creative platform showcasing recycled art and crafts with stories and poetic narratives. It promotes sustainability, environmental awareness, and creative reuse through interactive digital exhibitions.

Collaborations & Invitations

The Craft Thriller is open to collaborations, workshops, artist residencies, exhibitions, conferences, and skill exchange opportunities centered on sustainability, creative reuse, and community engagement.

This project has been designed to travel lightly — adaptable to classrooms, studios, public spaces, and digital platforms.

Open to invitations, sponsored travel, funded workshops, and exchange-based collaborations.
